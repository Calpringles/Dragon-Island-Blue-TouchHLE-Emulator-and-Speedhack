HOW TO PLAY DRAGON ISLAND BLUE ON WINDOWS
==========================================

This lets you play the old iPhone game Dragon Island Blue on a Windows PC.

It takes about five minutes. You do not need to be technical — just follow
the steps in order.

You need two things:
  * this folder (you already have it)
  * the game file, which ends in ".ipa"

Any reasonably modern Windows 10 or 11 PC will work.

This is a Windows build of the same modified touchHLE used on Mac, with the
same extra compatibility fixes needed to make this particular game work. The
plain touchHLE you can download from touchhle.org will NOT work for this
game — it crashes partway through loading. Use the touchHLE.exe in this
folder instead.


- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
STEP 1 — Put the folder somewhere permanent
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Move this whole folder out of your Downloads folder and somewhere you won't
accidentally delete it — for example directly on your C: drive, or in
Documents.

(If you skip this it still works, but the folder may vanish next time you
tidy your Downloads.)


- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
STEP 2 — Open it the first time (the fiddly bit — read this one carefully)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The first time you open touchHLE.exe, Windows WILL show a blue warning
screen saying "Windows protected your PC". This is normal and expected. It
happens to every app that wasn't downloaded from the Microsoft Store.
Nothing is wrong.

Here is how to get past it.

  1. Double-click touchHLE.exe. A blue screen appears saying "Windows
     protected your PC".

  2. Click the small text that says "More info".

  3. A new button appears: "Run anyway". Click it.

The app now opens. You will never have to do any of this again for this
copy of touchHLE.exe.

  If Windows instead just silently refuses to open the file, or your
  antivirus quarantines it, that's your antivirus being overly cautious
  about an app it doesn't recognise. You may need to restore it from
  quarantine or add an exception — check your antivirus's own instructions
  for that.


- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
STEP 3 — Give it the game
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

When it opens, you will see a mostly empty screen saying "No apps found in
the .\touchHLE_apps directory". That is correct — you haven't added the
game yet.

  1. Click the "File manager" button at the bottom of the window.

  2. A File Explorer window opens, and touchHLE closes itself. This is
     deliberate.

  3. In that window, double-click the folder called "touchHLE_apps".

  4. Drag your game file (the one ending in ".ipa") into that folder. Make
     sure it goes directly inside "touchHLE_apps", not inside another
     folder within it.

  5. Open touchHLE.exe again. The game is now shown as an icon. Click it,
     and it starts.

That's it. From now on, playing is just: open touchHLE.exe, click the game.


- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
PLAYING
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The mouse is your finger. Click where you would tap. Click and drag where
you would swipe.

To make the window bigger, drag any edge or corner of it. The game was made
for a phone screen, so it will look softer the bigger you make it. That is
the game's real resolution, not a setting you can turn up.

To stop playing, close the window.


- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

"touchHLE crashed with an error about an unimplemented function."
    This is the exact problem this Windows build was made to fix. Make sure
    you're running the touchHLE.exe from THIS folder, and not one you
    downloaded separately from touchhle.org — the official version doesn't
    have the fix for this game.

"The game isn't showing up after I copied it in."
    Make sure the file ends in ".ipa", and that it is directly inside the
    "touchHLE_apps" folder rather than inside another folder within it.
    Then close touchHLE completely and open it again.

"Where are my saves?"
    In the same folder the "File manager" button opens, inside a folder
    called "touchHLE_sandbox". Copy that folder somewhere safe if you want
    to back up your progress.
